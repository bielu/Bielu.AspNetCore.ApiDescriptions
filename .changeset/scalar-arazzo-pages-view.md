The Arazzo workflow overview in Scalar has a Pages view for readers who follow a journey screen by screen. With the new `pagesExtension`, `pageExtension` and `labelExtension` options (`ScalarArazzoOptions.PagesExtension`, `PageExtension` and `LabelExtension` in `WithArazzoOptions`), a document that describes its pages and labels its steps and actions gets a "Technical | Pages" toggle: steps are grouped into page bands, boxes and arrows read in plain language, and end nodes are named after the page they show. Technical stays the default, and the choice is remembered in the browser.
