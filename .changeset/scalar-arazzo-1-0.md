Release the Arazzo plugin for Scalar (`@bielu/scalar-arazzo`) as stable `1.0.0`. It shows every field of Arazzo 1.0 and 1.1, which spec each step targets, links from each step to its operation or channel, AsyncAPI message payloads, collapsible steps, a workflow and step filter, and works from the keyboard and at phone width. From 1.0.0 it follows semantic versioning; the rendered markup and CSS classes are not part of its public surface.
