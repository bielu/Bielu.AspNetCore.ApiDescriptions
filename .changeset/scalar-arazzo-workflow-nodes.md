The Arazzo workflow overview in Scalar draws workflows as nodes of their own: a step that invokes a workflow, and one node per workflow that `goto`/`retry` actions invoke, with an arrow back to the step for a `retry`. A workflow of the same document can be expanded in place to show its steps, recursively, with a guard against cycles, and a step that invokes one can list that workflow's steps inline. `end` failure actions that can be told apart get end nodes of their own, named after the action or after the value of a configurable extension (`WithArazzoOptions(arazzo => arazzo.EndLabelExtension = "x-page")`, `createArazzoPlugin({ endLabelExtension })` or `scalar-plugin.mjs?endLabelExtension=`), and the last step without `onSuccess` now leads to Success, since Arazzo ends a workflow successfully after its last step. Actions without criteria are labelled "otherwise", with the action's name in the label's tooltip. The boxes under a step are in a fixed order (failure paths left in document order, then next steps, Success rightmost), which expanding or collapsing a workflow never changes, and the toggled workflow stays under the pointer at the same zoom.
