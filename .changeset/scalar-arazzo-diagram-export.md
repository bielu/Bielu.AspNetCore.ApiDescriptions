The Arazzo workflow overview in Scalar can be exported, from a download button beside its expand button (inline and full window): as an SVG or a PNG of the overview as shown, expanded workflows and theme included, or copied as a Mermaid `flowchart TD` (a `.mmd` download when the clipboard isn't available) to paste into Markdown that renders Mermaid.
