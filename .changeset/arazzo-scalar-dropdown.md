The Arazzo workflow overview's export menu is Scalar's own `ScalarDropdown` (from `@scalar/components` 0.35.1), so it looks and behaves like Scalar's menus: arrow keys move through the items, Enter or Space picks one, and Escape, Tab or a click outside closes it. The plugin bundle grows from 344 KB to 413 KB (minified).
